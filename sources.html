APPENDIX 2

Learning Source Types

Twelve kinds of online learning sources, each with a Cowork-specific example you can open from this page.

1

YouTube videos

A video-sharing platform where independent creators, professionals, and companies publish tutorials, walkthroughs, demos, and explainers. For learning a new AI platform, YouTube is often the fastest way to see how something works.

Cowork example

Introducing Cowork: Claude Code for the rest of your work — Anthropic's official launch video, a polished short showing what Cowork looks like in motion.

2

Official article or training video

A written piece or recorded session published by the AI company itself, usually on their main website, help center, or events page. Authoritative; sometimes marketing-flavored.

Cowork example

The Future of AI at Work: Introducing Cowork — Anthropic's official webinar with two product leads walking through the vision, demos, and the kind of multi-step work Cowork is designed for.

3

Community discussion

A thread on Reddit, Hacker News, or a similar online forum where users share experiences, ask questions, and debate the strengths and weaknesses of a tool. Less polished than official content; often more honest.

Cowork example

First impressions of Claude Cowork — a Hacker News thread where technical users dissect what Cowork does well, where it falls short, and how it compares to other agents.

Cowork example

r/ClaudeAI — the main Claude subreddit, where users post questions, tips, and discoveries about Cowork and other Claude products on a daily basis.

4

Use case from a professional in my field

A write-up in which someone in your industry describes how they actually use the platform in their day-to-day work. The most directly transferable form of learning.

Cowork example

Claude Cowork for Legal Teams: Contract Workflows and Oversight — Spellbook's brief for lawyers on using Cowork for NDA triage, contract redlining, and intake routing, with discussion of ABA Rule 5.1/5.3 supervision obligations.

5

Comparison review between two AI platforms

A side-by-side analysis from someone who has used both. Useful for figuring out which tool fits which job.

Cowork example

Do You Really Need Claude Code If You Already Have Cowork? — a hands-on comparison of Claude Code vs. Cowork from a writer who has tested both extensively.

6

Official documentation page

The platform's own structured reference material, usually organized by feature. The source of truth — typically authoritative and frequently updated.

Cowork example

Cowork Overview — Claude Docs — the canonical Cowork overview in Anthropic's official documentation. Defines what Cowork is, the architecture, and the relationship to plugins, skills, and connectors.

7

Release notes or "what's new" page

A running log of recent product updates — what was added, changed, fixed, or removed. The fastest way to keep current with a moving product.

Cowork example

Claude Release Notes — Anthropic's consumer-facing release-notes page covering all Claude apps, including Cowork updates (Windows availability, model upgrades, scheduled tasks, plugins, connectors).

8

Podcast episode

A recorded audio conversation, often a long-form interview with someone building or using the tool. Good for context, philosophy, and behind-the-scenes detail you do not get from documentation.

Cowork example

Why Anthropic Thinks AI Should Have Its Own Computer — Felix Rieseberg of Claude Cowork & Claude Code Desktop — Latent Space's long interview with Anthropic's Cowork engineering lead, covering the origin story, sandboxing philosophy, and where local-first agents are headed.

9

Substack essay or newsletter

A long-form piece on the Substack platform.

Cowork example

Claude Cowork, clearly explained (and how to use it) — Tayla Burrell's thorough setup guide, covering folder structure, the CLAUDE.md and MEMORY.md files, global instructions, and worked examples. Closer to a workbook chapter than a blog post.

Cowork example

Cowork. — Ruben Hassid's piece in "How to AI," a less didactic, more reflective essay on what Cowork changes about daily knowledge work.

10

Medium article

A piece on the Medium publishing platform.

Cowork example

Claude Cowork vs ChatGPT: Why I Switched (And What I Actually Miss) — Vinayak Ramesh's first-person account of moving daily knowledge work off ChatGPT onto Cowork — what was better, what he missed, and how the file-based context model changes the rhythm of working with AI.

11

LinkedIn long-form article

A full-length post on LinkedIn, distinct from short status updates.

Cowork example

How to Get Started with Claude Cowork as a Marketer — part of Robert Gillespie's "Marketer's Guide to Claude" series, framing Chat as your whiteboard and Cowork as your doer, with concrete marketer workflows.

12

Personal blog post

An essay on a writer's own website with a custom domain.

Cowork example

Building Websites with Claude Code — Dr. Leon Furze's write-up of building five complete websites and one complex interactive page using a mix of Cowork and Claude Code, including downloadable Claude skills.
