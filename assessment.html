3. AI Partnership

AI Partnership is the shift from issuing one-shot instructions to having a conversation — treating AI as a working colleague designed to think through a task alongside you.

Focusing on the past 30 days, here are chat behaviors I engaged in. Examples:

3.1

Engaged in a thoughtful, back-and-forth conversation with my AI partner and produced a better output than had I simply drafted a long prompt and hit "submit"

3.2

Gave my AI partner specific, positive feedback and asked my AI partner to incorporate the positive output into its memory — talking through what specifically worked so the memory captured the underlying principle

3.3

Gave my AI partner specific, constructive feedback and asked my AI partner to adjust its memory to help prevent the same issue from arising in our chat — walking through together what had gone off-track and what the better approach looked like

6. Analytical Workflow Design

Analytical Workflow Design is the practice of defining the analytical objective up front — what to look for, what would matter, what would be missed — before asking AI to work across a set of documents like contracts, depositions, or due diligence materials.

Describe how you would design an analytical workflow for a project in which AI is asked to review a body of documents, such as a set of deposition transcripts, a stack of contracts, or a due diligence binder. Think about what would matter most in the documents and what would be easy to miss. What would you tell AI to flag rather than merely summarize? This is a hypothetical, so feel free to invent facts.
