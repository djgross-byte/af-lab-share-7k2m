SELF-ASSESSMENT

AI Fluency Self-Assessment

This is for you, not for us. You will not turn it in. The questions are here to help you think about your own work with AI — what you already do well, what you might try next. There are no right answers.

1. Operational Fluency

Smooth handling of everyday AI session mechanics.

2. Learning From AI

Turn to AI itself when you want to get better at AI. See Appendix 2 and Appendix 1.

3. AI Partnership

Shift from one-shot instructions to conversation.

4. Step by Step

Pace the session one move at a time.

5. Memory Architecture

Capture and reuse productive sessions.

6. Analytical Workflow Design

Define the analytical objective before reviewing documents.

7. Don't Trust. Verify.

Ask AI to show its work; catch hallucinations.

8. Objection. No Foundation.

Lay groundwork before the substantive question.

9. Levels of Abstraction

Start at the high level before diving into details.

10. Humans Still Matter

Judgment, experience, and knowing what matters drive AI success.
